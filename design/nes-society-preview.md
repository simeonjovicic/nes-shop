# NES Society invitation — 2026-09-30

A compact newsletter-popup prototype with a personal-invitation concept. A photograph, the small “NES Society” label, a heading, one short introduction and an email form carry the design. Bone-coloured paper, olive accents and contrasting serif typography introduce “Ein guter Kreis. Ihr Platz darin.” / “Good company. Your place in it.” A newly generated portrait, `nes-society-salon-v1.webp`, shows two adults in conversation in a quiet Italian salon. The photograph is displayed without text overlays; the mobile crop keeps both faces in the banner. See [the asset record and exact generation prompt](nes-society-photo-prompt.md).

The email form validates locally, then shows a short welcome message and a continue button. Following the user's request for a simpler popup, the benefit list, image lettering, decorative button arrows and member-preview card have been removed, and the desktop dialog is smaller. The preview does not call the subscription endpoint, save an email address, send analytics events or create a membership. The form and welcome view explain that this is a preview. The display flag is held only in React memory, without cookies or browser-storage writes. The existing footer newsletter remains independent.

The invitation appears after 12 seconds on the homepage, once per page load (retained through internal navigation). An open bag, mobile menu, enquiry form or legal dialog postpones it. Hidden tabs and active form fields suppress the timed opening. “NES Society” in the footer opens it manually from any page. `/?society=preview` opens it immediately for design review, even after dismissal.

The native modal dialog makes the background inert. The shared dialog-focus hook keeps keyboard navigation inside; closing restores focus to the opener. Escape, the close button, “Vielleicht später” and the backdrop dismiss it. The welcome button also closes it. Body scrolling is locked while open, with the page position retained. Small screens use a short image banner and a scrolling modal when necessary; reduced-motion preferences remove the entrance animation.

Implementation: `src/SocietyInvitation.jsx`, `src/society-invitation.css`, and the invitation trigger/state in `src/App.jsx`. Copy is available in German and English. A future real integration should replace the local submit handler with the existing confirmed-subscription flow and update the preview copy to the actual offering.

Validation covers local email validation, the welcome view, no form requests or email storage, keyboard focus and restoration, all dismissal methods, once-per-page-load timing, homepage-only automatic opening, other-dialog suppression, German/English, reduced motion and layouts from 320px through 1440px, including short landscape viewports.
