The idea

N.E.S brings several of the best barefoot brands together in one place — each chosen for foot health, natural movement, and design.

Barefoot means a wide toe box, zero-drop construction, and a thin, flexible sole — shoes that let your feet do what they were made to do.

The brands

Green Comfort

Denmark · Est. footwear house

A Danish footwear house with more than twenty years of craft, built around deep comfort and everyday well-being. Premium, long-lasting shoes made from sustainable natural materials — organic cotton, natural rubber, recycled content — and produced ethically. Scandinavian design at its most honest: understated, refined, made to last.

Vehon

Formerly WAI · Engineered barefoot

A modern, forward-looking barefoot brand with a sleek, almost futuristic identity. Minimalist barefoot construction — zero-drop, wide toe box, ultra-flexible — engineered for natural movement with a clean, contemporary look.
